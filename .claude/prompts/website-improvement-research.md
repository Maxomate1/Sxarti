## Task: Target Audience Research & Website Improvement Strategy for Sxarti

### Context

Sxarti is an AI-powered sales automation SaaS for Georgian small businesses.

- Customers chat via Facebook Messenger/Instagram
- AI bot (Gemini) handles full sales flow in Georgian
- Business owners manage orders from a dashboard
- Revenue: monthly subscriptions (Starter ₾49, Business ₾149, Premium ₾299)

### Phase 1: Research Target Audience Needs

Using web search, investigate:

1. **Georgian SMB landscape** — pain points of small businesses in Georgia (country), especially in e-commerce, social commerce, and customer service automation
2. **Competitor analysis** — what similar tools (Tidio, ManyChat, Chatfuel, local Georgian solutions) offer that Sxarti doesn't
3. **Social commerce trends in Georgia** — Facebook/Instagram shopping behavior, messaging-based sales patterns, payment preferences
4. **AI chatbot adoption barriers** — what stops small businesses from adopting AI tools (trust, language, cost, complexity)

### Phase 2: Audit Current Website/Product

Review the existing Sxarti codebase to understand:

- Current landing page and marketing pages
- Feature set exposed to users
- Onboarding flow
- Pricing presentation
- Dashboard capabilities
- Mobile responsiveness

### Phase 3: Brainstorm Improvements

Using brainstorming skill, generate categorized ideas:

1. **Quick wins** — small changes with high impact (copy, UX, trust signals)
2. **Feature gaps** — missing capabilities competitors have or users expect
3. **Growth levers** — viral loops, referral mechanics, integrations
4. **Retention boosters** — features that reduce churn
5. **Georgian market-specific** — localization, payment methods, cultural fit

### Output Format

Deliver a prioritized list of improvements with:

- Category (quick win / feature / growth / retention / localization)
- Description of the improvement
- Expected impact (high/medium/low)
- Implementation effort (small/medium/large)
- Rationale tied to research findings
